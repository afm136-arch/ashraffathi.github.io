Clause 2 · The record

Ten years of full P&L at the world's largest travel retailer

Country Managing Director at Dufry Group across Egypt and Kenya, with every commercial, operational, regulatory, financial and people function owned simultaneously, across Cairo International, Sharm El Sheikh, Borg El Arab and Asyut airports and the Kenya operation, serving over 15 million international passengers a year.

2.1

The tender

Won a competitive tender for a 3,000+ sqm concession at Africa's busiest international airport against strong international competition, leading full commercial preparation, bid strategy and negotiation, then delivered +20% revenue growth in Year 1.

2.2

The legislation

Identified that Kenya's trade legislation was incompatible with modern duty-free operations, drafted the replacement framework with PricewaterhouseCoopers, presented it to Parliament and secured its passage. It was adopted industry-wide across all operators in the country.

2.3

The contract

Restructured a standard 4-page concession contract into a 72-page commercial framework covering passenger-flow disruption, currency fluctuation and force majeure, protecting operator revenue across the full concession lifecycle. This page is typeset in its honour.

2.4

The build

Built the Dufry Kenya operation from zero under complex security and regulatory conditions: government and authority relationships, customs frameworks, brand-partner agreements, supply chain and team infrastructure. Launched Africa's first Hudson News store at Cairo International in 2009, and sustained 12% year-on-year revenue growth over 7 years, with 25% market share expansion across the Egyptian portfolio.
